# NutriMate AI

NutriMate AI is a smart nutrition scanner, meal tracking, grocery list, and healthy habits application built natively for Android using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

## Key Features

- **NutriBot AI & Assistant**: Real-time evidence-based nutritional insights, macronutrient calculations, glycemic index details, and bilingual clinical guidance (Telugu & English).
- **Food Scanner & Recognition**: CameraX integration for analyzing food items and logging nutrition metrics.
- **Comprehensive Food Database**: Verified nutrition facts for regional and global foods including calorie breakdowns, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, and health alternatives.
- **Interactive Meal Logging & Tracking**: Breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner logs with daily calorie and macronutrient targets.
- **Hydration & Water Reminders**: Daily water tracking with customizable reminders and alarms.
- **Streaks & Habit Building**: Streak tracking and community challenges to maintain healthy dietary habits.
- **Bilingual Experience**: Full support for both English and Telugu languages.
- **Local Persistence**: Powered by Android Room database for local, offline-first data persistence.

## Tech Stack

- **Platform**: Android (minSdk 24, targetSdk 36)
- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose & Material Design 3
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Kotlin Coroutines & Flow
- **Local Storage**: Room Database
- **Networking & Media**: CameraX, Coil
- **Testing**: Robolectric, Roborazzi
